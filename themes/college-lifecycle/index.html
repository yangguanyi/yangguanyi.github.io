Research/Human capital

Research theme

Human capital, household choices and labor-market risk

How education choices, household resources and labor-market risk interact over a lifetime. These papers examine flexible college access, schooling disutility, college majors and temporary employment. Work on time inequality and household dynamics is in progress.
