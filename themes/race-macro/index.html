Research theme

Racial disparities in labor markets and wealth

Quantitative macroeconomic research on racial differences in labor-market opportunities and wealth accumulation. The available working paper studies hiring discrimination in a search model; a separate project examines monetary policy and fiscal transfers in a HANK model.
